Property Description

Character homes of this calibre are a rare find in Riverhead. Jasmine Villa sits on a generous 807m² freehold site on a quiet no-exit street.

A covered verandah with a classic bullnose roofline, ornate fretwork and extra-wide entry stairs sets the tone. Mature hedging, established trees and granite pavers frame the home, and the quiet setting adds to the sense of retreat.

Inside, soaring high-stud ceilings, leadlight windows and a calm contemporary palette create a light-filled home. The hard work is done: fresh interior paint, new drainage, designer lighting, a moisture barrier, underfloor and ceiling insulation, and ducted heating and cooling to every room.

Four generous bedrooms include an oversized master with a bay window and walk-in wardrobe. Outside, a covered entertaining area and oversized rear deck overlook established gardens and lawn, with a workshop, garden shed and lit attic storage.
